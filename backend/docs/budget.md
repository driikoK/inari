# Budget («Печивко»)

Part of the backend guide, see [CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md).

- Budget (`src/budget/`): money is stored as whole kopiykas (`amountKop`), every sum is derived from the journal on read, nothing is cached. The summary and the running balances do not load records: `loadGroups` asks Mongo to count entries that are equal in every field the sums depend on (`$group` with `count`), and `calc.ts` takes those groups (`EntryGroup`, a plain entry counts as one). A new field that changes a sum must be added to that `$group` key. A record has a `date` (Kyiv, never in the future) and a `season`+`year` that is chosen on its own and may differ from the date. `calc.ts` holds the pure maths (`effectsOf`, `diffEffects`, `balancesOf`, `summarize`) and is where a new kind of record must be added. An order splits as `general = round half up (10%)` into the jar and the rest into the budget of its title (`splitOrder`); a title is keyed by its project id, or by its trimmed lower-cased name when typed, and a lisodar by its trimmed lower-cased name. The jar is `income + gifts to the development of the team - support - title share of orders - payouts` and the coefficient is `(jar before payouts) / 100 / crumbs the team earned in the season`, where the crumbs are the sum of `track.coins` by season from `TrackService.getCoinsBySeason`, guests and hidden members included, so payouts never move it. The coefficient is rounded down to four digits (`coefficientOf`, integer maths) and a payout is rounded down to a kopiyka (`kopOfCrumbs`), so what is paid at it never adds up to more than the jar, and the last payout of a season can empty its jar to exactly zero. A season without crumbs has `coefficient: null`. `GET /budget/summary` gives `seasons`, which is always the previous and the current season (in that order, even when empty; the home page and the cards read it), and `earlierSeasons`, every older season that has an entry or crumbs in it, the newest first, each the same `SeasonSummary` it would be as the previous one, so its coefficient is the rate a payout of that season is paid at (`seasonRates`). A season that has not come and a season with nothing in it are not listed. A project chosen for a record gives it its name (looked up once per project in a batch). If that project was deleted later, editing the record keeps the name it already has and a payout takes the name its budget already has (the newest record of that project), while a new income or another unknown project is still 404. The name shown for a budget is the one on its newest record (`lastDate` of the group), not the first one the database returns. The journal counts a record with no `type` as an income, in the type filter and in what it returns. The journal is sorted by date, creation time and `_id`, the last one so that pages never repeat or skip a record. Only `GET /budget/summary` and the routes of `budget/requests` for a member (`mine`, `preview`, asking and cancelling one's own line) are for every logged-in user, the journal, the writes, the audit and the queue of requests (`GET /budget/requests`, `GET /budget/requests/open-count`, `fulfil`, `reject`) are admin-only. `open-count` is the number of open lines (crumbs and orders, payable or not) that the menu shows next to «Видатки». The server does every sum a screen shows, including the totals of the popups (`supportAllocatedKop`, `supportSpentKop`, `ordersReceivedKop`, `ordersSpentKop`) and what a new set of support budgets would take out of a jar (`POST /budget/support/preview`, read-only, same body as `PUT /budget/support`), so the frontend adds and subtracts nothing.
- Budget records have a `type`: `income`, `allocation` (money moved from the jar of a season into a support direction, the amount is signed and a negative one gives it back) and `expense` («Частування», a payout `card` / `donation` / `development` out of a direction or out of the budget of a title). Every record is a list of effects on sides (`effectsOf` in `calc.ts`): jars, title budgets (`orders:`), directions (`support:`), and the slices (treats, lisodari) that only show where money came from. The jars, the title budgets and the directions hold all the money of the team and add up to the confectionery, so a record that only moves money (an allocation, a gift to the development of the team) keeps that sum, and only a card or a donation takes money out. A gift to the development of the team lands in the jar of the season of its date as a lisodar, which is the only expense that touches a jar. When a record of a new kind is added, give it effects and the sums follow, and add every field the effects use to the `$group` key of `loadGroups`. `summary.ts` builds the cards, the breakdowns of the popups (directions, title budgets, treats, lisodari, incomes by source) and the top of expenses (a season by the date of the payout, the orders together, each direction, the gifts to the team left out).
- The support budget is set with `PUT /budget/support` by saying what each direction should hold: the difference to what it holds now (allocated minus spent) becomes an allocation in the season the admin chose and comes out of that jar, or goes back into it. A direction that did not change gets no record. A payout is a `POST /budget/expenses` of up to 20 lines with one date and one comment (the season is the one of the date) and may be edited with `PUT /budget/expenses/:id`. A card number is checked (exactly 16 digits, then the Luhn sum, which is Zod's own `z.creditCard()` in `budget/card.ts` and in the frontend `Budget/const/card.ts`, that one alone takes 12 to 19 digits so the length stays ours; a number of 16 digits whose check digit does not agree gets its own message about the check digit, so «16 цифр» is only said about a wrong length, and the frontend `expenseForm.ts` says the same) and only its last four digits are ever stored, in the journal and in the audit; a donation takes a card or an https link, a link is kept as it is, and an edit sends the masked number back (a masked number is refused when a payout is created, as nothing checked it). A whole card number is refused in every free-text field too (comment, name, title, a link): `hasCardNumber` looks for 16 digits with spaces or dashes between them that pass Luhn.
- Withdrawal requests (`requests.service.ts`, `budget_requests`, routes `budget/requests`): a line is one section of what a member asked for (`kind` card, donation or development), the lines of one form share a `groupId`. A line is `open`, then `done`, `rejected` or `cancelled` (by its owner). A request for crumbs holds `crumbs`, a request for an order (`isOrder`) holds `amountKop` and a title that already has a budget. The account asks with the nickname linked to it (`user.nickname`), a user without one is refused with 409. `withdrawal.ts` has the pure maths: `sharesOf` (the seasons of a nickname that are finished and have a coefficient, oldest first, from `BudgetService.seasonRates`) and `planWithdrawal` (takes from the oldest season first, each part at its own coefficient; a part that would pay under a kopiyka is dropped and its crumbs stay with the nickname). Open lines of a nickname reserve their crumbs, so a request is refused when more than `balance of the finished seasons − reserved` is asked, and the preview (`POST /budget/requests/preview`) answers with the same check and the price instead of refusing, and also refuses a line that would pay under a kopiyka. When nothing can be paid out, the answer says why, season by season (`lockedSeasonsOf` and `nothingToWithdraw` in `withdrawal.ts`): a finished season with crumbs whose jar has no money (no coefficient, the admin has to enter incomes into that season), the season that is still running, or all of it already held by open lines. The check reads the coefficients and the budgets of the titles once (`BudgetService.requestFacts`). A line is only as good as its account still holding the nickname (`UsersService.nicknamesOf`): when an admin unlinks or deletes the account, or links the nickname to somebody else, the line stops reserving crumbs, the queue shows it with a problem and «Виконано» refuses it with 409, so the admin rejects it. The admin's `POST /budget/requests/:id/fulfil` runs in the queue of `BudgetService.exclusive`: it closes the line first (the atomic claim keeps a second click from paying twice), takes the crumbs off the nickname season by season with `MembersService.changeCoins` (guarded against going below zero), then writes one payout per season to the journal (`section: 'crumbs'`, `crumbSeason`/`crumbYear` of the crumbs, `crumbs`, `coefficient`, `requestId`, `season` of the date of payment) and audits all of it as one `request.fulfilled` operation, whose rows are the jars and the crumbs of the nickname. `BudgetService.commit` checks the jars before anything is written, and when a later step fails the crumbs are put back, the payouts removed and the line opened again. If the crumbs or the payouts cannot be put back, the line stays closed and the answer is 500 with what to fix by hand in the rating, because opening it again would take the crumbs a second time. A payout of crumbs comes out of the jar of the season of the crumbs, a gift to the team (`development`) also puts the money into the jar of the season of its date and makes a lisodar, and a payout of crumbs cannot be edited or deleted in the journal (400), as the crumbs have already left the nickname. A card number is kept whole only in an open line, which only the admin can read (`GET /budget/requests`); once the line is closed, and everywhere else (the member's own list, the journal, the audit), only its last four digits remain. A closed line is listed for the season it was closed in and the one after it (`REQUEST_HISTORY_SEASONS` = 2, `historyStart` in `requests.service.ts`, the same window for the member's list and the admin's), and after that only the journal and the audit remember it. It is never deleted, because the payouts of the journal point to it by `requestId` and the reconciliation reads the requests (a deleted fulfilled request would show up as a payout that belongs to none). `GET /budget/requests?crumbsPage=&ordersPage=` answers `{ crumbs, orders }`, each `{ open, closed }`: `open` is every open line of that kind (never in pages, they are the work and the menu counts them), `closed` is a page of `QUEUE_PER_PAGE` = 10 of the lines closed in the window, the one closed last first (`Paged`, so `total` and `perPage` come with it), and the two kinds have a page each. An order is fulfilled with the sum the member asked for or the one the admin corrected it to, and the crumbs are not touched.
- Nothing in the budget may go below zero: `commit` in `BudgetService` refuses with 409 and the numbers («Недостатньо коштів у «Підтримка: Сайт»: є …, потрібно …») when an operation takes more from a side than it holds, whatever the operation is, so a payout, an allocation, and also deleting or moving an income whose money was already given away. Only a side that gives money in the operation is blamed. All writes take turns in one in-process queue, so two payouts at once cannot spend the same money (the server is one process, a second instance would need a real lock).
- Budget audit: every create, update and delete of a record writes rows to `budget_audit`, one row per side the operation touched (a jar, the budget of a title, a treat of a project, a lisodar of a person), rows of one operation share an `operationId`. A row has the `actor` (the username), the subject, the `season` and `year` (those of the jar for a jar row, which can differ from the season of the record when it was moved, else those of the record), the signed `deltaKop`, `balanceAfterKop` and the `before`/`after` of the record. `BudgetAuditService` can only record and read, keep it that way and add no route that changes or deletes. The audit lives in `BudgetAuditModule` (the service, `CrumbAuditService` and the model) so that `TracksModule` and `MembersModule` can use it without importing `BudgetModule`, which imports `TracksModule`. A row is `unit: 'money'` (`deltaKop`, `balanceAfterKop`, old rows have no `unit` and are read as money) or `unit: 'crumbs'` (`deltaCrumbs`, `balanceAfterCrumbs`, `seasonBalanceAfterCrumbs`, subject `crumbs:<nickname>`). The server has no Mongo transactions (a single node), so the record is saved first and the audit second. When the audit fails the request answers 500 «Операцію виконано, але запис в аудит не збережено» with `error: 'AUDIT_NOT_SAVED'` (`BUDGET_ERROR`, mirrored in the frontend `types`) and the record stays, so a screen must treat it as saved. The audit page groups rows by operation with `allowDiskUse`. Never put a full card number into an audit row.
- Reconciliation (`reconcile.ts` has the pure checks, `reconcile.service.ts` reads the sources, `GET /budget/reconcile` and `POST /budget/reconcile/opening`, admin only): `GET` adds everything up again and answers a report of ten checks (`RECONCILE_CHECK`), each with a status (`ok`, `warning`, `error`), its number of problems and the first 50 of them. A problem with numbers carries `expected`, `actual` and `difference` in kopiykas or crumbs as its `unit` says, so the frontend subtracts nothing. It runs inside `BudgetService.exclusive`, so a record of money that is saved but not audited yet is not taken for a gap, and it writes nothing. Crumbs are written outside that queue (tracks, the rating), so a change of crumbs in the middle of a run can show as a gap until the next run. The checks: the confectionery against incomes minus card and donation payouts (told without effects, so a wrong effect shows), nothing below zero, the rows of money in the audit against the journal by side, every record has a row in the audit, every record is whole (integer kopiykas, a payout of crumbs equals its crumbs times its coefficient), every fulfilled request has its payouts and the rows of crumbs that were taken off the nickname and every payout belongs to a fulfilled request (a line a failed fulfilment left closed shows up here), the crumbs of a nickname against the sum of its seasons, not below zero, against the rows of its audit (only for nicknames with `crumbsAuditedAt`; every row is rounded to a thousandth while the counter is not, so the tolerance is half a thousandth for each row, and the sums carry their number of rows; nicknames that differ only in case are added up, as the audit keys them by their lower-case name), and per season against what the tracks gave minus what was withdrawn (a warning: a manual edit in the rating makes it differ, and the message quotes the manual changes of the audit). `POST /budget/reconcile/opening` writes the opening state (`MembersService.auditOpening`, the same claim as a first change) for the nicknames that have crumbs and no `crumbsAuditedAt`, signed with the admin, and answers the nicknames it wrote.
