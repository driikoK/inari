# Members, accounts and tracks

Part of the backend guide, see [CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md).

- `root` can be neither edited nor deleted, and nobody can delete their own account. Deleting a user also deletes their paws in open proposal seasons (`ProposalsService.deleteParticipationsByUser`), the paws in closed seasons stay. Tracks keep `username` as plain text.
- Members ("nicknames") are soft-deleted with `deletedAt`. Every member listing must filter `deletedAt: null`. `findMember(nickname)` deliberately still finds hidden members so their old tracks can be edited and deleted. `createMember` restores a hidden nickname with its coins and merges the roles. Creating a track for a hidden nickname is refused with 409 before anything is written.
- A member has a `kind`, `team` or `guest` (`MEMBER_KIND`). Nicknames created before the field have none and count as not marked, which is a normal state, not an error: `GET /members?kind=unmarked` lists them. `POST /members` requires a kind and applies it when it restores a hidden nickname. The kind changes only through `PATCH /members/:id/kind`, `PUT /members` strips it. Nothing on the backend turns a guest into a guest track line, the frontend ticks `isGuest` per line and the half-coins rule stays per track.
- `GET /members/coins-total` (`season` and `year` together or neither, `includeGuests` defaults to true) is the sum shown above the Rating table. It is its own aggregation and ignores the role, kind and nickname filters of the list. It skips hidden members, a season takes the `seasons[]` coins and only nicknames that have that season, and without a season it sums `coins`. `includeGuests=false` drops only `kind: guest`, so a nickname without a kind stays in as team, and `unmarkedCount` says how many of those the total holds. `includeGuests` is parsed with `z.stringbool()`, `z.coerce.boolean()` would turn `'false'` into true.
- A user account is linked to a nickname through `User.nickname` (plain text, unique and sparse), set only by `PUT /users/:id/nickname` (`{ nickname, create }`), never by `PUT /users/:id`. `null` unlinks. A nickname that is hidden (`deletedAt`) or held by another account is refused with 409 and `root` cannot be linked. An unknown nickname is 404 unless `create` is true, which makes a `team` member with no roles and 0 coins. Hiding a member does not clear the link, `GET /members/me` just answers an empty balance for it (`nickname: null`). `GET /members/me` is for any logged-in user and answers the linked nickname's `coins` plus the coins of the current season (`seasonOfDay(todayInKyiv())` from `projects/layout.ts`), never anybody else's.
- `UsersService` injects `MembersService`, so `UsersModule` imports `MembersModule`. `TracksModule` provides `UsersService` itself and already provides `MembersService` next to it, keep both there.
- Tracks store the member's nickname as plain text, and creating, editing or deleting a track adjusts the member's `coins` and the matching `seasons[]` total. Coin rates and multipliers live in `DictionariesService` and `tracks/enums`.
- `POST /tracks/bulk-delete` (`{ ids }`, 1 to `MAX_BULK_DELETE` = 200 ids, admin only) removes many tracks as one operation, all or nothing. `TrackService.bulkDelete` takes each id once, skips the ones that are gone already (`skipped` in the answer), takes the crumbs off first with one `changeCoins` for every nickname and season, and when a nickname has too few in a season it puts back what it took and removes nothing (409 naming the nickname and the season). The audit is one `track.deleted` operation with a row per track. The balance after each row runs down (the final balance plus the crumbs of the tracks after it in its group), so the rows of a nickname still add up for the reconciliation. The answer is `{ deleted, skipped, coins }`, and the sum is the backend's. The one race left is the same as in deleting one track, another admin removing the same track at that moment, and it is logged. `GET /tracks` sorts by `createdAt` and then `_id`: tracks written in one moment share their time, and without the second key a page that cuts through them repeats a track on the next page and loses another.
- Crumb counters change only through `MembersService.changeCoins`, never by reading a member, adding and writing it back. It is two atomic `$inc` updates (the season that exists, else a `$push` of a new one), so two requests at once cannot overwrite each other, and it takes `delta` and the `actor`. Unless the caller passes `guard: false` (undoing its own change) a negative delta is refused with 409 when the total or the season would go below zero. There are no transactions, so `TrackService` changes the counters first, then writes the track, and puts the counters back when the track write fails. `PUT /members` still sets the total and the seasons by hand, now as one `findOneAndUpdate` that returns the old values, and leaves roles and seasons alone when they are not sent.
- Every change of crumbs is audited (see the audit rule in [budget.md](budget.md)): `track.created` (one operation for a whole track, a row per nickname), `track.updated`, `track.deleted`, `member.created` (a new nickname with starting crumbs) and `member.adjusted` (`PUT /members` that changed the total or a season, not one that changed only the roles). The actor is the logged-in account, never the `username` in the body. Seeds pass `SYSTEM_ACTOR`. A nickname that was already there when auditing began gets `crumbsAuditedAt` and an opening row (`crumbs.initial`, its whole state then) from its first change: whoever wins an atomic claim on `crumbsAuditedAt` writes it, before the change, so the rows of a nickname always add up to its total. A new nickname gets `crumbsAuditedAt` at birth. Anything that changes crumbs must go through `MembersService` and `CrumbAuditService`, or the sums will not match.
- A text that puts a number before «крихти» (an audit description, the `problem` of a request) uses `formatCrumbsWithUnit` of `budget/calc.ts`, never `${formatCrumbs(x)} крихт`. `crumbsWord` gives «1 крихта», «2 крихти», «5 крихт», «11 крихт», «21 крихта», and «крихти» after a fraction («1,5 крихти»), judging the number the way `formatCrumbs` shows it. Audit rows keep the text they were written with, so old ones still say «1 крихт».
