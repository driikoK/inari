# Home page, crumbs page and the track form

Part of the frontend guide, see [CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md).

## Home page

- `src/pages/Home/` (`/home`). A guest sees the greeting and «Увійти» only. A logged-in user sees, top to bottom: the greeting, `AboutBlock` (nine plain lines about the sections, «Видатки» among them for everyone as «Крихти» is, always open, no accordion; the line of «Лисяча комора» is hidden when `VITE_SHEET_EMBED_URL` is empty), `SectionLinks` (the sidebar items of the user's role without «Головна» and «Налаштування», from `useNavItems`), `StatsGrid` with the cards and the tops.
- The numbers need no backend of their own: members from `/members` («Учасники»: team and guests by `kind`; the nicknames without a kind are counted only for `EDIT_MEMBER_KIND`, as «не позначено»), the current season of `/budget/summary` (`isCurrent`; «Сезонна банка печива» is `jarKop` with the incomes in the hint, «Видано печива» is the sum of the season's `expenses` lines, i.e. every payout of the season without the transfers to the development of the team, «Топ надходжень» and «Топ видатків» are the five biggest lines) and the current season of `/projects` (`stats`, drawn by the `SeasonStats` of the Projects page). «Топ крихт» is the old leaderboard: all time, every nickname, shown from three members on.
- All seasonal blocks are for the current season only, there is no picker. `plural` (`utils/plural.ts`) declines «лис» and «гість».

## Crumbs page and the track form

- `src/pages/Cookie/` (`/cookie`) has three cards: add crumbs (`InputCookieDialog`), add a member or a guest (`AddMemberDialog`) and create a project (it opens the `ProjectFormDialog` of the Projects page). Titles are not added from here any more.
- The first step of the track form (`ChooseAnimeForm`) picks a project from `useTitlesStore`, grouped «Триває» and «Завершені» and found by the Ukrainian, the romaji or the code name. The type is the project's, shown as text. The season is a select of the last three years of seasons (back to the start of the project when it began earlier), newest first, set to the season of the start by default, and the crumbs go to the one picked. Its menu is capped at 220 pixels and scrolls (`MenuProps` in `slotProps.select`), because thirteen options would be taller than the dialog. The previous episode (`getLastTracks({ projectId, episode })`) only pre-fills the team and the subtitles-only mark. The payload carries `projectId`, `season` and `year`, and the server fills in the title and the type.
- The list of crumbs filters by project and shows the name the project has now (`titleNames`), falling back to the `nameTitle` the track was written under. Its filters (`CookiesFilters`) are nickname, project, role and one season. They are not state of the component: `useTracksStore.appliedFilters` holds the filters and the page together, so they are still there when the admin comes back, a new filter always asks for page 1 (the page the admin was on may not exist in the shorter result), and the table takes its page from the store. `getTracks` drops the answer of a request that is not the latest, because filters changed quickly do not answer in order. Nickname and project are `SearchSelect` (`components/SearchSelect`, found by typing; an option can carry a `hint`, `keywords` and a `group`, and the project is found by its Ukrainian name, its romaji name or its code name, with the running ones grouped above the finished). The season is `SeasonFilter` (`components/SeasonFilter`), one select of «Весна 2026» from `FIRST_SEASON_YEAR` to the season that is going on, the newest first, which also means a season and a year cannot be filtered apart. The Rating uses the same two components, so the lists of years are not kept by hand in two places.
- Bulk delete on the list is for `SUBJECTS.COOKIES_LIST_DELETE` (admins): a checkbox column (`checkboxSelection`), `selectedIds` in `CookieList` (not in the store) and `keepNonExistentRowsSelected`, so what is ticked stays when the page or a filter changes and can be more than the table shows. The community DataGrid has no `checkboxSelectionVisibleOnly`, its header checkbox ticks the rows of the page. `BulkDeleteBar` above the table (`role="status"`, so find it by its text in tests, the toasts have that role too) shows the count with «Скинути вибір» and «Видалити вибрані». More than `MAX_BULK_DELETE` (100 in `utils/constants.ts`, which is the lower of the two: the backend takes up to 200) is refused as a whole with a toast, because the grid sends the whole new selection. The confirmation is `ConfirmDeleteDialog` with `autoFocusCancel` and names only the number of tracks, the sums are the backend's and come back in the toast (`deleted`, `skipped`, `coins`). `deleteTracksBulk` in `useTracksStore` reloads the list and, when the page it was on is gone, asks for the last one that is left. A refusal (409) keeps the selection so it can be changed, a success clears it.
